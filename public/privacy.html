Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Wealth Tracker is a personal, single-user web application for tracking an ETF portfolio and net worth. It has no backend server and no user database.

Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This grants access solely to a hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see, read, or modify any other file in your Drive, your email, contacts, or any other Google data.

Where your data is stored

  • In your browser (IndexedDB) on the devices where you use the app.
  • In the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive, as a backup and for sync.

Your data is never sent to the app developer or to any other server. The only external services contacted are the Google APIs (authentication and Drive) and a public exchange-rate API (api.frankfurter.dev), which receives only currency codes and dates, never your financial data.

Use of Google user data

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to store and synchronise your own portfolio data. It is not shared, sold, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and tracking

The app uses no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

Sign out from the app menu and clear the site data in your browser to remove local data. To remove the Drive copy and revoke access, open Google Account → Third-party connections, select this app and choose Delete all connections; you can also delete the app's hidden data from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Contact
