Proposed scope and next decision

Recommended layout: an optional Settings card and one secondary, initially collapsed Net worth card. No new main navigation tab, chart, combined headline, forecast or monthly-entry workflow. Settings remains discoverable when there is no data.

Small record: label, use, whole-property estimate, owned percentage, valuation date and personally attributable mortgage principal/date, or explicit debt-free/unknown status. Enter your own debt directly, rather than configure loan rates, payment allocations or amortization. Ownership and debt need not have equal shares.

Examples: an inherited home can be debt-free; a build uses present as-is land/work value and drawn debt, never finished value or the approved facility. Two or three properties use the same list, with no rental accounting. A rented-property label does not produce income estimates.

History boundary: this is a latest-known informational register, not historical property performance. No reconstruction of past ownership, inheritance returns, purchase flows or mortgage amortization is attempted. Values retain their actual dates. Unknown debt prevents a complete equity total; negative equity stays signed.

If approved for production: implement a small separately stored register, explicit validation and errors, and deliberate SQLite persistence, backup/restore, Drive sync and compatibility tests. Do not use device-only localStorage as a shortcut. Keep it outside account snapshots and existing financial calculations. Removing a record would remove it from this register only; there is no sale transaction or automatic cash movement.

Not implemented: any of that production storage, real data entry, history, reports or app wiring. The current deliverables are this disposable interaction pilot and the roadmap findings. Approval should establish that occasional manual estimates are acceptable and that the small card is useful enough to keep.

2. What mortgage debt do you still owe?
Sharing a mortgage, or unsure what to enter?

Enter only the remaining debt you count as yours. If the remaining loan is EUR 280,000 and your agreed split is half, enter EUR 140,000. Your debt split may differ from your ownership share; we do not calculate it automatically.

A monthly payment includes interest and repayment of borrowed money. We only need the borrowed amount still unpaid, not all future payments added together.

If you are unsure, leave the amount unknown. We will not assume you are debt-free. Your agreement for tracking shared debt does not change what the lender can legally require you to repay.
